## 3. Request to a health-facility in-charge / waste-management focal person

**Subject:** Request for a short introductory meeting — healthcare-waste records improvement prototype

Dear [Facility In-Charge / Waste-Management Focal Person],

I am a nursing student developing SmartMed-UG, a phone-based prototype that records healthcare-waste batches, handovers, transporter/document references, and treatment/final-disposal entries while offline. It is designed to show missing documentation—not to judge staff, replace the facility's official records, or certify safe disposal.

I am asking whether you would be willing to have a brief introductory discussion to understand your existing waste-recording workflow and whether a supervised evaluation might be useful. No patient records or names are needed. I would not enter real facility data, photograph waste, change waste handling, or start a pilot without your written permission and any required university, research/ethics or district approvals.

If you are open to the discussion, I would like to learn which records are authoritative, which waste categories and evidence references your SOP requires, who should review the workflow, and what data-protection steps would be necessary. The prototype's local database is not yet encrypted at the application layer and its role labels are not login credentials, so we would first need to agree on an appropriate safe test approach before any operational data is entered.

Would a short meeting with the facility in-charge and designated waste focal person be possible? A meeting would not commit the facility to participate.

Kind regards,
